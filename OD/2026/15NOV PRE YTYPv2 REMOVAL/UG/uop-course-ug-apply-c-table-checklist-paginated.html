Questions about applying

Can I apply directly instead of through UCAS?

Yes. Use our online application form if you'd rather not use UCAS.

What if I miss the 13 January deadline?

You can still apply through UCAS. We'll consider your application if we still have places. From July, you can also apply through Clearing.

I'm a mature student. Can I apply?

Yes. We welcome applications from mature students, and we look at your experience as well as your qualifications.

When will I hear back?

You'll see our decision in UCAS Hub. We'll also email you about Applicant Days and next steps.

What happens after you apply

  1. We review your application

    We'll look at your grades, personal statement and reference.

  2. You hear back through UCAS

    Our decision appears in UCAS Hub. We may invite you to an Applicant Day.

  3. You reply to your offers

    Once you've heard from all your choices, pick your firm and insurance choices.

When you accept an offer to study at the University of Portsmouth, you also agree to our Student Contract (including the University's relevant policies, rules and regulations). Please read and consider these before you apply.
